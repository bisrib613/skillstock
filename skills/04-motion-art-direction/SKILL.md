# Motion Art Direction — Prompt Specification

## Purpose
Translate commercial utility into motion that communicates a process, state, interaction, transformation, or useful visual system.

## Research-derived motion utility
Prioritize stock-useful formats:
- isolated/alpha-capable transitions and elements;
- dashboard/UI overlays;
- seamless background/data loops;
- explainer B-roll components;
- concise promotional motion.

Research places commercial motion in a 5–15 second range. For this project, actual duration is selected only by project rules and user request; never copy a duration from a research example.

## Motion stack
For each meaningful scene/state define:
1. purpose;
2. entry state;
3. primary action;
4. supporting movement;
5. transition;
6. exit state;
7. timing;
8. easing/velocity character;
9. settling/hold;
10. loop relationship when applicable.

## Premium motion language
Research contrasts robotic linear interpolation with custom cubic-bezier/spring behavior, secondary motion, and micro-animation. Use acceleration/deceleration, anticipation, settling, restrained secondary motion, and readable staging. Linear motion is acceptable when constant speed is functionally appropriate.

## Instant comprehension
The main state/action must remain immediately readable. Do not bury it under particles, camera effects, excessive transitions, or decoration.

## Loop
For loops, final state must return deterministically to initial state. Match position, rotation, scale, phase, and other persistent procedural variables.

## Output
Return concrete motion states, timing, movement hierarchy, easing, transitions, and commercial utility.
