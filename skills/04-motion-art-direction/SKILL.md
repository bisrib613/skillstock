# Motion Art Direction

Design motion that functions as stock footage, overlays, or explainer components.

Prefer:
- non-linear easing
- cubic Bézier/spring-like behavior
- anticipation and settling
- useful secondary motion
- readable pacing
- clean transitions

Avoid:
- constant linear interpolation
- gratuitous particles/effects
- excessive camera movement
- unreadable rapid sequencing

Consider seamless loops, alpha-friendly isolation, short editorial utility, safe zones, and clear start/end states.
