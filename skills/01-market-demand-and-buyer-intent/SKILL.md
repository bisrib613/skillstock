# Market Demand & Buyer Intent

Translate themes into evidence-aware commercial opportunities.

Consider:
- evergreen vs current vs seasonal/emerging
- buyer role
- actual use case
- functional communication value
- differentiation
- production feasibility

Common buyer mappings from the research:
- web/advertising: horizontal composition + useful copy space
- presentation/report: modular, neutral, adaptable components
- vertical social video: immediate readability + safe zones
- explainer/overlay: isolated elements + alpha usefulness

Do not claim rankings, sales probability, or exact demand without supporting evidence.
